Hero Mission: The Healthy Choice

Play the game · Source repository

Game rule

Each run randomly samples 10 questions from a built-in bank of 17. Every question presents two choices. Correct choices add one point; missed questions store their associated advice for the final briefing.

question → two choices → score/advice update → repeat 10 times → score out of 10 → final briefing

Topics

The bank covers vaping and nicotine, unknown pills, peer pressure, drug offers and secrecy, trusted adults, stress, exercise, lungs, smoking effects, hobbies and personal body control.

Activation context

This game belongs to the same kids-* game set developed for the multi-game interactive children’s edutainment activation in the UAE.

Health-information boundary

The question and advice text is simplified game content. It is not a complete medical, substance-use-prevention or public-health curriculum, and the score is not a validated measure of health knowledge, risk, behavior or future substance use.
