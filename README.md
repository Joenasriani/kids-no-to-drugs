# Hero Mission: The Healthy Choice

Play: https://kids-no-to-drugs.vercel.app/

Hero Mission: The Healthy Choice is a 10-question drug-prevention and personal-safety quiz game.

## How it plays

The game contains a bank of 17 two-choice questions. Each run shuffles the bank and selects 10 questions.

**random question → choose one of two responses → update score/store missed advice → repeat 10 times → final score and briefing**

Correct answers add one point. Incorrect answers store the question's advice text. The final screen shows the score out of 10 and combines the score band with missed-advice feedback when applicable.

## Topics

The current bank includes vaping and nicotine, unknown pills, peer pressure, drug offers, secrecy, trusted adults, stress, exercise, lungs, smoking effects, hobbies and personal control over one's body.

## Content note

The questions and advice are simplified prevention/safety game content, not a complete medical or public-health curriculum and not individualized medical advice.
